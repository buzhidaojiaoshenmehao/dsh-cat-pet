# 小爪同伴的动作素材

使用内置 imagegen 参照猫的形象生成两张透明 RGBA 图集。图集保存在 `assets/atlases/poses.png` 和 `assets/atlases/walk.png`。运行 `scripts/prepare-sprites.mjs` 会保留透明度，切分为 `bin/assets/` 中的 10 张 PNG。

## 六个动作姿势

参考 `01-front-master.png`、`06-curious-paw.png`、`07-relaxed-loaf.png`、`08-belly-up.png`，按顺序作为提示词中的 #1 到 #4。

> Create ONE production sprite atlas for a Windows desktop pet using the actual pet in the provided photos. True transparent RGBA background. Landscape canvas, exactly 3 equal columns × 2 equal rows, six separate full-body cutouts with large clear transparent gutters. All subjects fit strictly inside their cell with at least 25 pixels of clear padding on every side. No boxes, grids, labels, props, ground, contact shadows, outlines, logos, text or scenery. Keep the exact same chubby adult brown-black mackerel tabby, white blaze, muzzle, bib, belly and socks, golden amber eyes, pink nose, caramel-brown little chin patch, and thick ringed dark tail. Preserve natural photo texture, breed, proportions and asymmetric markings. Use the images as identity and pose references: #1 front seated, #2 curious lifted paw, #3 round loaf, #4 belly-up. From left to right on TOP: front seated like #1 looking toward viewer; alert 3/4 seated with slightly tilted head and curious wide eyes, all four paws grounded naturally; paw raised like #2. From left to right on BOTTOM: sleepy loaf based on #3 with eyes gently shut; playful relaxed belly-up based on #4 with curled paws and content eyes; low crouch angled left, shoulders forward, focused on an imaginary point, ready to play. Keep each individual distinct and centered in its cell, full visible tail, no overlapping cutouts. Soft diffuse lighting and clean alpha around whiskers.

## 走路循环

参考 `03-profile-standing.png`、`01-front-master.png`，按顺序作为 #1 和 #2。

> ONE 2×2 sprite atlas, true transparent RGBA background, four full-body side-profile walk frames of the SAME pet cat in the supplied photos, facing LEFT in every cell. Photoreal natural fur cutouts for a desktop pet. Exact equal quadrants, each cat centered and comfortably inset from every cell edge by ample transparent gutter, all paws and the entire tail visible. No labels, arrows, box lines, floors, shadows, props, or scenery. #1 is the strict side-view reference. #2 is the same cat's face identity reference. Lock the face, chubby long body, relative torso size, viewing angle, tail length, framing and light across all 4 poses. It is the same adult brown-black mackerel tabby, white muzzle, chest, belly and white socks, amber eye, pink nose and little caramel chin patch, thick ringed tail. Four different natural phases of one slow four-beat feline gait, in reading order: top-left near front paw reaching forward and near hind paw back; top-right near front paw planted below shoulder and far rear foot reaching, a passing pose; bottom-left far front paw reaching forward and near rear paw under the belly; bottom-right near front paw lifting into the next stride and near rear paw planted. Keep paws at common baseline, consistent head and shoulder center relative to each cell, all four limbs anatomically plausible. Shape and scale of the cat must remain consistent across all four. Crop nothing.
